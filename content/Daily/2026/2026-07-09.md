The `weight` value corresponds to the original Mobius strip triangles. All other simplices had their weight set to 1.

The input vector is the one I found before:
$$
\mathbf{b} = [3,6] - [0,4] + [0,3] + [0,1] + [6,7] + [2,4] + [1,2] + [7,8] - [2,8] + [3,8] + [2,3] - [0,8]
$$
but with $\lambda=10^{-5}$. Visually $\mathbf{b}$ is the given below (blue arrows):

![[SS_2026-07-09_1783631135.png#invert | center]]

The optimal solution was:
$$
\begin{align*}
	z &= 3.00009 \\
	\mathbf{x} &= \frac{1}{2}[3,4] + \frac{1}{2}[0,1] + \frac{1}{2}[1,2] + \frac{1}{2}[4,5] + \frac{1}{2}[2,3] - \frac{1}{2}[0,5] \\
	\mathbf{y} &= \frac{1}{2}[3,4,6] + [0,4,6] - \frac{1}{2}[0,3,6] - \frac{1}{2}[1,4,6] - \frac{1}{2}[0,1,6] - [4,6,7] \\
	&\phantom{=}+ \frac{1}{2}[1,4,7] - [2,4,7] - \frac{1}{2}[1,2,7] + \frac{1}{2}[4,5,7] - \frac{1}{2}[5,7,8] - \frac{1}{2}[2,7,8] \\
	&\phantom{=}- \frac{1}{2}[2,3,8] - \frac{1}{2}[0,5,8] - \frac{1}{2}[0,3,8]
\end{align*}
$$

The 1-chain $\mathbf{x}$ just wraps around the Mobius strip but with weights $1/2$:

![[SS_2026-07-09_1783631357.png#invert | center]]

Or visually (where $\textup{red triangle}=\pm\frac{1}{2}$ and $\textup{green triangle}=\pm1$):

Testing the chains in Python shows that
$$
	\mathbf{x} - [\partial]\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{b},
$$
but
$$
	2\mathbf{x} - [\partial](2\mathbf{y}) \ne \mathbf{b}.
$$
So we can't make it a valid homologous chain by simply doubling the chain coefficients! Rounding doesn't work either!
